About Suncat

Suncat is an independent music producer and game developer based in Houston, Texas. Alongside a vast archive of original rock, pop, and avant-garde albums, Suncat Studios produces immersive video game soundtracks.

You can hear the latest game audio in action by playing Runestones Online, a free, browser-based tarot card battler and dungeon crawler developed by Suncat.
